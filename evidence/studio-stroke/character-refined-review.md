Image-only review of draft-fullres.png and draft.png.

The character reads immediately as a friendly person waving. The raised arm has a clear bend, the open hand is recognizable, and the hand, wrist, sleeve, and shoulder form a continuous gesture. The head and neck also connect clearly to the body. I see no serious recognition or connection failure that would prevent this from working as a simple flat cartoon.

The main shape weakness is the body silhouette. The broad, almost horizontal shoulders and long, straight torso feel rather rigid beside the more organic head and waving arm. The hanging arm also runs close to the torso for much of its length, giving that side a heavy, column-like shape. This is a refinement of character and pose, not a broken attachment.

The raised hand is readable but visually lighter and more delicate than the rest of the figure. Its very thin outline, narrow fingers, and lightly drawn palm details contrast with the stronger outlines around the hair and clothing. The wrist-to-cuff transition is slightly pinched. At thumbnail size the palm detail disappears, but the waving silhouette survives.

The hair is recognizable as a blue bob. The pointed lower side locks and the nearly straight dark section behind the neck make it feel a little helmet-like. The close parallel curves around the side locks can also look like extra contour strokes. These are styling refinements, not confusion about what the object is.

The face is friendly and legible. The heavy upper eyelids and relatively tiny irises give a mildly sleepy expression; the eyes also feel slightly different in their opening and tilt. This is noticeable in the large image but does not spoil the expression in the small image.

The palette is coherent and pleasantly restrained. Hair, skin, clothing, and background separate well. Flat coloring suits the illustration; added shading is not needed to make it usable. Line weight is the less settled part: delicate hand and facial marks sit against much stronger hair and garment contours. A more deliberate hierarchy would make the whole character feel more unified.

Overall: usable, clearly connected, recognizable flat character with a successful waving gesture. The remaining issues are proportion, contour rhythm, and line-weight refinement rather than serious structural defects.
