# Editable gestures and a connected character

## Question and controlled comparisons

Point mounts prevent placement drift; they do not supply a useful elbow, a sweeping vane or a
convincing sleeve. This unit compares existing mass controls with a simple rounded gesture mark.
The default must work without path coordinates; optional controls must support visible revision.

- 01-existing-cactus is the unchanged independent four-mass cactus.
- 02-shape-tuning changes only existing rounding/width controls. It softens the tip but keeps the
  segmented elbow. This is the prior-art control, not a new primitive.
- 03-default is the new round mark; 04-spine-push applies a real bounded midline correction.
- 05-round-strokes-cactus replaces the two branch blocks with two rounded marks. Fresh critics
  prefer its outer continuity but retain the heavy arm and inner notch.
- 06-curved-vane-pinwheel replaces the former petals with swept/tapered strokes and colour planes.
  Both fresh critics see a stronger consistent turning direction; narrow crowded roots and a
  ribbon-like reading remain. This is not a physical folded-paper solution.
- 07-single-gesture-cactus uses one continuous bowed branch instead of an assembled elbow. Its
  fresh image review finds a coherent minimal icon and no serious connection defect. It remains
  a flat silhouette with a schematic pot, not a volumetric observational plant.

comparison-review-a.md and comparison-review-b.md are fresh image-only comparisons. The later
character-cactus-review-b.md reviews the single-gesture cactus separately. Root-authored changes
are not independent creator transfer evidence.

## The actual character revision loop

08-character-first is a frozen six-component prototype. character-working.json and its history
are the actual working source, not a replaced initial example. Source uses a head, a neck mark,
one blouse mass, two sleeve gestures and an open hand. Chin/wrist and explicitly inspected
shoulder points attach them. No target image, generated pixels or manually authored visible cuts
are used. Independent depth remains separate from point connections.

09 inspects a terminal. 10 shortens the neck; 11 extends the blouse through the crop without
thickening its ink. 12/13 inspect both right-side mounts and raise the shoulder. 14 is the old
sample-stepping inspection; 14b refreshes it after continuous interpolation. 15 raises the other
shoulder and 16 extends its sleeve beyond the crop. These accepted proposals are retained with
images and their original candidates. 17-character-check supplies the first fresh review pair.

Both first critics recognize the wave but identify a tube-like raised arm, a redundant thin
opposite sleeve and an oversized blank torso. The response changes the construction rather than
claiming that mathematically valid mounts settled the picture: 18 gives the blouse clearer shoulders;
19 removes the isolated dark tube stripe and tapers/eases the raised sleeve; 20 widens the relaxed
sleeve and puts it in front so it reads as an arm. Each clean proposal was actually viewed before
CLI acceptance. 21-character-check contains the refined picture. This remains a bounded flat
character study, not whole-portrait acceptance or an anatomy rig.

## Operation and validity

Stroke length/thickness are independent. Local spine pushes protect both terminals and nominal
width; free-terminal moves preserve the other terminal. A pinned terminal remains fixed; a center
pin cannot silently drift. Optional colour planes follow the same strip/caps; they are graphic
material artwork, not a lighting/fold solver. Invalid crossings, stalls and collapsed paint refuse
before acceptance. Public contract and commands live in docs/7_INTERFACE.md and the drawing skill.

Chin is the head's own median-axis contour intersection, not the lowest sampled vertex (the latter
actually shifted the joint sideways and was corrected). Wrist shares the generator's wrist datum.
Positioned side@fraction mounts interpolate points/normals continuously; nearest-station snapping
was visibly coarse and was replaced. Local protection compares the actual pinned point and normal.
Candidate closeups follow where the selected feature lands while preserving prior selection binding.

A valid author intent containing markup and double hyphens initially produced invalid XML. SVG
comments now escape text and normalize comment hyphens, while raw authored intent/IDs are retained.
The regression covers all nine kinds. This is a real text-input fix, not a drawing-quality claim.

## Next useful gap

cannot preview removal/replacement of an obsolete component. That is a concrete revision gap for
