# Fresh image-only review

Viewed only `17-character-check/draft-fullres.png` and `17-character-check/draft.png`.

## Overall read

The picture reads immediately as a simplified, friendly blue-haired person waving. The face, hair, raised palm, and clothing remain legible in the small image. It does not need realistic anatomy to work in this graphic style.

## Main issues

1. **The raised arm reads like a curved hose attached to the side of the torso.** Its long U-shaped bend has almost uniform width and no clear upper-arm/elbow/forearm organization. At the shoulder it emerges from the torso's outer side relatively low, rather than seeming to grow from the shoulder mass. The gesture is understandable, but the body connection is the largest weakness.
2. **The opposite arm is difficult to identify.** On the viewer's left there is a narrow sliver with an extra small bump near the shoulder and a long tapered strip down the side. At thumbnail size it could be a garment fold or duplicated torso contour. Its shoulder and sleeve boundary do not explain themselves.
3. **The raised sleeve-to-hand transition feels cut and pasted.** The sleeve ends with a slanted flat boundary against the wrist, while the inner dark stripe stops abruptly there. The wrist is much narrower than the sleeve and its two edges do not feel fully integrated into that ending. This is a smaller issue than the shoulder/elbow construction, but it reinforces the assembled-parts impression.

## Secondary issues

- The raised hand is readable, with a useful thumb gap, but the fingers look like very thin parallel sticks compared with the broad palm. The thumb-side wrist/palm contour has a pronounced step. A little clearer knuckle/palm organization would strengthen it without requiring detailed anatomy.
- The dark shading on the inside of the raised arm forms a continuous broad stripe around the bend. It emphasizes the tube shape and does not convincingly explain a sleeve fold or change of plane.
- The torso is an extremely large, blank rounded block, cut off at the image bottom. The head and waving arm carry essentially all the character information. This can be intentional in an icon, but the unresolved arm sliver makes the blank body feel less deliberate.
- Hair has a nice recognizable overall mass, but the straight dark back-hair lower edge behind the neck reads somewhat like a rigid panel. The two pointed side locks help, although their thin ends have a slightly different degree of sharpness.
- The eyes have heavily dark upper shapes and visible lower outlines, while nose and mouth are much lighter. This gives the expression a slightly heavy-lidded look. It remains pleasant and readable; this is a style choice rather than a blocker.

## What is already working

The face is coherent, the neck visibly joins the head to the shirt, the waving hand is separated clearly from the head, and the warm shirt/skin against blue hair is easy to parse. Outlines are generally controlled. There is no major color readability problem. The priority is to clarify how both arms attach and bend, especially the raised shoulder and elbow, rather than adding facial detail or texture.

## Severity

There is no blocker to recognizing the intended character and gesture. For a finished character drawing, the arm construction and the ambiguous opposite arm are the substantive issues. Finger proportions, back-hair shape, eye emphasis, and blank torso treatment are secondary refinements or style preferences.
