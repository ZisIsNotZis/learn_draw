# Usability drawing

Recognition: A smiling, dark-haired person in a teal top, raising an open hand in greeting. This is immediately legible at both sizes. The fingers and thumb read as a hand; I do not see an extra or missing digit.

Connection and pose: The raised sleeve connects to the torso and the hand connects to the sleeve. However, the arm is so compressed beside the oversized head that the gesture reads more as a small hand attached near the shoulder than an articulated raised arm. That is acceptable for an avatar, but it gives little evidence of an elbow or a convincing human arm pose. The torso has no visible opposite arm, which further makes this an icon rather than a complete upper-body figure.

Shape and line: A tiny pale downward triangle beneath the hair on the viewer-right shoulder looks like an accidental gap. It has no clear anatomical or clothing meaning. The hair sides finish in long sharp points, but these remain readable as stylized hair. The broad horizontal hair base, very large head, compact neck and trapezoid torso belong to the same simplified cartoon vocabulary; they are not in themselves connection failures. Fine palm lines disappear in the thumbnail, without compromising recognition.

Color: The warm skin, dark violet hair, teal clothing and cream background separate clearly. Flat fills suit this drawing; lack of shading is not a defect. The dark hair and dark outlines are somewhat heavy relative to the delicate face, but the face remains readable.

Overall: A clear greeting avatar. Its principal limitation is the compressed arm anatomy; the small light shoulder notch is the clearest local cleanup issue.

# 21-character drawing

Recognition: A blue-haired person in a muted orange top, smiling and waving. The raised hand and greeting pose are clear at full resolution and thumbnail size. Five digits read correctly.

Connection and pose: The neck, head, torso, shoulders, sleeves and raised hand form a coherent connected figure. The raised arm follows a readable bend from shoulder to hand. Its elbow is a broad, smooth curve rather than a joint, giving the sleeve a soft tubular quality; this is a cartoon simplification, not evidence that the hand is disconnected or the arm points impossibly. The visible opposite arm makes this pose more bodily convincing than the usability avatar. Both the torso and opposite arm continue out of the bottom crop; this is understandable as framing, although it leaves the lower pose unresolved.

Shape and line: The long, narrow neck and extended torso make the body feel stretched relative to the head. The rounded exposed neck bottom also looks a little like a tab laid over the shirt because there is no distinct collar opening. The raised sleeve meets the wrist at a fairly abrupt angled cuff, but the attachment is readable. Hair ends are pointed and graphic, consistent with the rest of the treatment. The eye shapes and eyebrows have slight asymmetry; this gives some expression and does not obstruct recognition. At thumbnail scale the face is small and loses detail, while the silhouette and hand survive well.

Color: Blue hair contrasts well with the warm shirt and skin. The restrained flat palette is coherent. There is no need to add gradients or volume shading merely to make this cartoon valid.

Overall: A readable, connected waving figure. Its main weakness is proportion and the tab-like neck-to-shirt transition, rather than a failed gesture or broken anatomy.
