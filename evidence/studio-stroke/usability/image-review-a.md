# Image-only review

Viewed draft-fullres.png and draft.png only.

The image is immediately recognizable as a smiling, dark-haired person waving. The raised hand, open fingers, and face survive the small preview. The large head and compact torso read as a deliberate cartoon proportion. I do not see a serious broken attachment, extra digit, or impossible pose.

The main weakness is the body-to-arm construction. The raised teal sleeve joins the torso in a broad, somewhat shapeless sweep; the shoulder and elbow are difficult to locate. It reads as a waving arm because of the hand, but the arm itself has little structure. The hand meets the sleeve coherently, although its narrow wrist and the much wider sleeve amplify this uncertainty.

The other arm is absent from the silhouette. This can work for a simplified bust, but the rounded, nearly symmetric lower body reads more like a bell-shaped shirt cutout than a person with a resting arm. This is a refinement issue for this simple cartoon, not a failure of recognizability.

The hair ends around the shoulders are the least tidy drawing detail: dark pointed ends and tiny pale triangular interruptions along the lower hair edge make that boundary look slightly accidental. The broad horizontal lower edge behind the face also makes the hair feel like a rigid panel. The long side locks still clearly frame the head.

The face is readable and friendly. Its eyes are unusually wide and horizontal, with heavy upper lids and small visible irises, so the expression is slightly sleepy or sideways-looking rather than an energetic greeting. This is stylistic, not a serious facial defect. The very fine nose, mouth, and hand creases lose definition at preview size, while the eye outlines remain dominant.

The restrained teal, purple, peach, and cream palette works. Flat colour is appropriate here; shading is not needed to make the picture function. Outline treatment is inconsistent: the torso, hair, and fingers have visible contours, while much of the raised sleeve has no comparable outline. This contributes to the sleeve feeling softer and less constructed than the rest.

Overall: a coherent, recognizable simple flat waving portrait. The most useful refinement is a clearer shoulder/arm silhouette and a cleaner hair-to-shoulder boundary. None of the observed issues makes the waving pose unreadable.
