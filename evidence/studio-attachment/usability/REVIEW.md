## Actual decisions and friction

- `11-arm-form` declined: positive 55 degrees descended to the right, opposite the intended branch gesture. `12-arm-form-rising` corrected the angle to -55 after the full preview was read. This was a discoverable sign convention rather than a blocker.
- `20-tip-mount` declined: the oval tip produced a pinched, leaflike joint. `22-tip-column` changed the tip to a shorter, broader rounded column before remounting. This illustrates the skill's stated boundary: a pin places one point; it does not smooth the joint or make a cactus solid.
- `25-tip-mount` was refused because inset .65 is outside [-.5,.5]. The skill and CLI help explain inset units but omit its range. The error was clear and recovery to .5 was immediate. No ready candidate or PNG existed for the refused attempt; the raw error is in commands.log.
- Keeping every current-revision pair required fresh inspections after unrelated accepted edits. This is coherent and reliable but creates a noticeable CLI interaction cost for four simple masses. I did not bypass it.
