- The stick tapers into a small rounded lower tip while keeping nearly parallel sides for most of its length; the change near the bottom looks a little abrupt at full size.

At thumbnail size the silhouette is clean, colors remain distinct, and nothing is visibly clipped or missing. The broad empty margins are balanced. The main weakness is the ambiguous blade form, not legibility or layout.
