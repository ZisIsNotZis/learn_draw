# Independent image review B — coast

Viewed only draft-fullres.png and draft.png from coast-final-check. Asked: what is wrong here?

## Actually visible

A flat-colour coastal illustration: a pale sun at upper left, level blue sea, a cream lighthouse at right with red roof and yellow lantern panes, and a large dark rock in the lower-right foreground. Both images display the same complete composition; neither is blank.

## Concrete visual defects

- The foreground rock is cropped heavily by the bottom edge and reads as a broad, nearly rectangular slab. Its small left facet meets the dark face in a sharp angular wedge, while the top is a soft rounded cap; these treatments do not quite agree about the rock's solidity.
- The lighthouse sits well inside a broad, smooth, olive-grey cap. There is no distinct grounding shadow or contact cue at its foot, so the base looks lightly pasted onto this flat patch.
- The tiny yellow lantern panes almost merge into one solid yellow block at thumbnail size. The darker centre mullion is legible in the large image but contributes little small-scale detail.
- The dark oval at the left horizon and the separate dark oval in the foreground have no clear connection to a shoreline or visible body. They read as isolated floating marks; their identity is ambiguous.
- The water highlights are very sparse and short. In particular, the two middle highlights look like isolated thin slits rather than a coherent pattern of reflections.

## Layout at thumbnail size

The lighthouse and sun remain immediately legible. The heavy cropped rock anchors most of the lower-right corner; the left half is notably emptier. This is a readable quiet composition, with the ambiguous oval marks and oversized smooth rock cap its clearest visual weaknesses. No claim here depends on an inferred implementation cause.
