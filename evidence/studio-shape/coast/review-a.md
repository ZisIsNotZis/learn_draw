# Independent image-only critique

Viewed only `check/draft-fullres.png` and `check/draft.png`. The detailed observations below come from the full-resolution image.

## What I see

A restrained, flat-colour coastal illustration. A pale sun sits in the upper left above a straight sea horizon. A tall cream lighthouse with a red triangular roof and yellow lantern windows stands right of centre. Its body has a darker right face, two small dark windows and a doorway. A large dark rounded landform lies beneath and to the right of it. A lighter grey angular rock overlaps that landform on its left. Pale foreground water or shore curves across the bottom; two isolated dark oval marks appear on the left.

The small image retains a clear lighthouse silhouette and the broad composition. Fine details on the tower become much less prominent, as expected at that size. Neither image appears blank.

## What is wrong here?

1. The dark base is spatially ambiguous. Its very smooth rounded outline and almost uniform colour read partly as an island and partly as an enormous cast shadow. The lighthouse meets it without a clear contact edge or ground plane, so its footing feels uncertain.
2. The large lighter rock overlaps the left front of this dark base, but has no interior face changes or contact cue. Its rounded trapezoidal silhouette can read as a flat patch laid over the island rather than a solid boulder.
3. The two isolated left-hand ovals are also ambiguous: they could be rocks, shadows or water markings. Their lack of above-water edges makes them look like flat holes or spots. This weakens the otherwise recognizable coastal setting.
4. The foreground's broad pale curve and the dark island boundary meet on the right without much description of the shoreline. The image has distinct colour regions but gives limited visual evidence of where water ends and solid land begins.
5. The tower's doorway is cut off exactly at the base, with no step or sill. Combined with the flat dark landform, this makes the entrance appear as a notch in the tower silhouette rather than an opening with depth.

The lighthouse and sun are immediately readable, and I see no obvious stray marks or broken outlines. The chief issue is the inconsistent clarity of solid ground, rock and water, rather than recognition of the main subject. No implementation cause or unseen reference requirement is inferred.
