# Independent image-only critique

Viewed only `check/draft-fullres.png` and `check/draft.png`. No scene, source, documents, or other reports were consulted.

## What I see

A quiet, flat-color coastal illustration: a pale yellow sun at upper left; a muted blue-green sky fading toward a straight horizon; a blue sea band; and a lighthouse toward the right. The lighthouse has a red triangular roof, a dark lantern frame with yellow panes, a cream tapered tower, two small dark windows, and a dark entrance. It stands over a large dark irregular form. A gray-green angular rock overlaps that form on the left. Two detached dark oval shapes sit farther left, one near the horizon and one near the bottom. A broad pale curved area fills the foreground.

The reduced image preserves the lighthouse and sun immediately. Smaller tower details remain present but become secondary.

## What is wrong here?

- The large dark form beneath the lighthouse reads ambiguously: it could be an island, a broad rock, or a shadow. Its smoothly rounded perimeter, very dark uniform fill, and lack of a distinct top/side transition give it a flattened, puddle-like appearance. The lighthouse does not gain a strong sense of grounded contact from it.
- The gray-green foreground rock has an unusually smooth, swollen outline for a rock. Its single uninterrupted fill gives few cues about its faces or weight. It reads partly like a tilted soft slab resting against the dark form.
- The broad pale foreground region is difficult to identify confidently as water, beach, or another surface. Its curved boundary and the isolated oval within it provide little material information. As a result, the spatial relationship among sea, shore, and the lighthouse's base is weak.
- The two isolated dark ovals look more like graphic spots than clearly situated rocks or islands. The upper oval is especially detached from surrounding surface cues.
- There is a strong rendering difference between the lighthouse's crisp architectural details and the very smooth, nearly featureless land/rock forms. The surrounding forms feel less resolved than the tower.

The restrained palette and simple silhouettes make the scene legible as a lighthouse illustration. The main weakness is the reading of the ground and coastal surfaces, rather than recognition of the lighthouse itself. These are visible observations; no implementation cause or unseen reference comparison is inferred.
