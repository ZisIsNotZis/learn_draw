# Independent image review: face and hand

Viewed draft-fullres.png for detail and draft.png for layout. The images are visible and depict a blue-haired face and a separate raised, open hand on a pale background.

What is wrong here?

- The hand is visibly disconnected from the head: its wrist terminates in a straight diagonal cut and there is no arm or body. It reads as a separate study or symbol, not a person raising a hand.
- The hand has five recognisable digits, but the palm is long and broad relative to the narrow fingers. The transition from the thumb into the wrist has an abrupt inward notch; this gives the lower thumb side an angular, pinched contour.
- The fingers appear uniformly rounded and tube-like. Their crease marks are faint and sparse, so the bends and knuckles have little visible structure. The thumb projects as a smooth diagonal lobe with little joint definition.
- The head is also a floating cutout: there is no neck, and the rear hair ends in a conspicuously straight horizontal edge just above the pointed side locks.
- The upper hair cap has a thin secondary contour closely following much of its outside edge. At full size it looks like an uneven doubled outline, especially near the sides.
- The large smooth face, tiny nose marks, and narrow nearly horizontal eyes give the face little dimensional information. The ears sit as small attached lobes beneath the side hair, with no inner detail.
- At thumbnail size both face and hand remain legible, but they look like adjacent isolated assets. The thin skin-coloured interior hand lines become hard to see.

No blank or missing picture was observed. These observations concern the visible drawing only; no source or implementation was inspected.
