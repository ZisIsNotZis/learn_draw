## Observe

- Body: wide egg/ellipse, center ≈(465,500), rx≈148, ry≈112, slightly rotated; bottom dips below waterline (drawn full, water covers it? no — body outline continues below waterline, so duck is IN FRONT of water; waterline crosses body silhouette but body outline stays visible down to ~y 610).

- Tail: small curl poking up-right from body ≈(575,425).
- Beak: two-part orange, in FRONT of head's left edge (head outline interrupted): upper beak rounded wedge ≈(205..290, 293..345) with nostril dot; lower beak smaller wedge below, mouth slightly open.

- Blush: soft orange ellipse ≈(398,328).
- Wing: ellipse ≈(497,492) rx≈65 ry≈48, rotated ~-15°, 3 feather curves inside sweeping left from wing's right end.
- Belly: darker-gold shading area at body's bottom, above waterline.

## Occlusion order (painter)

sky → water → ripples → body → belly shade → tail → head → head sheen → wing+feathers → beak (both parts) → eye stack → blush.
Key relationships: head over body; beak over head; water behind duck; ripples behind duck where they'd touch it.
