## Priority and asset choices (research recommendations, not milestone contract)

1. **High priority: visibly successful semantic construction/rig methods**, especially IK, taper, pivots/rigid groups, anchors/attachments, named slots/hooks, and carefully chosen stretch. They target meaningful figure anatomy/pose, currently unresolved in Ex32, and have plausible spatial adaptation. Study local code and relevant vendor source together; don't port Canvas painter order or reference-coordinate rig wholesale.
2. **High priority: composition and silhouette/art direction**, because the goal is an artistic, readable whole, not merely correct surface mechanics. Examine vendor static examples and transferable composition ideas; compare possible first visible products rather than presuming a micro hand image is best. Preserve 3D/depth constraints while learning composition.
3. **High later priority: style/material/palette and static/dynamic separation.** Vendor shows many concrete authored rendering strategies; apply after structural requirements are observable, not by blanket deferral or immediate texture addition. Motion should be evaluated for static form construction, posed variants, and shape response; exclude only beat/audio/film production where irrelevant.
4. **Cross-cutting: QA/review** as evidence and failure discovery, not numeric optimization or substituted perception. Preserve external user visual review gate and independent reviewer distinction.

**Keep/reuse:** existing vector/geometry vocabulary and named relation methods where they provide explainable constructive parts; 3D support and depth visibility as architecture, not a constraint to remove; spatial components as the core capability; useful completed work/evidence as evidence with its actual limitations. Maintain reference-free/compact-parameter provenance and explicit tests for attachment vs occlusion vs physical intersection.

**Isolate:** legacy flat 2D resolver, `figure_scaffold.py` / Ex32 composition as historical/probe material, old 2D assembly as legacy; vendor rig and flat painter-order rendering as comparative method examples. Do not patch the old picture in this learning sequence or mistake a historical example for a passing person.

**Replace only when demonstrated:** no evidence warrants replacing spatial visibility or the support-surface model with vendor's flat painter-order rig. Do not retire prior primitives/works on aesthetic assumptions; test whether a genuinely better method solves a defined visual construction problem and preserves transferable use. Replace a local method only with comparative visible evidence and explicit compatibility.
