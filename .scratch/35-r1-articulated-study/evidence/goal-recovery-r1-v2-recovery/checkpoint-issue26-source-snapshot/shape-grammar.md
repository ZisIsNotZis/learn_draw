Teacher artifacts `teacher-crop-registered.png`, `teacher-crop-focus.png`, `teacher-grid.png`, `teacher-region-windows.png`, and `teacher-observations.json` document correct source/crop registration and approximate viewing windows. They are labeled **rough observations**, not masks or contours. The crop shows a fitted light bodice with angled shoulder/neck opening, an attached puff sleeve cap flowing into an upper-arm tube and dark cuff, two folded collar leaves framing the source-visible neck skin, plus an asymmetric chest bow and descending tails. Runtime accepts only grammar values and generated geometry; it imports no observation script, image, crop, mask, or trace.

All A coordinates are crop-local pixels, y-up in authored scene; projection maps art to the registered crop view without resize. Four-point caps measure **hand-authored coordinate pairs that actually enter family constructors**, not output curve samples. Named ratios/offsets are editable geometry rules; all output curves are generated and guarded.

- `fitted_bodice_panel(shoulder_left, shoulder_right, hem_right, hem_left, side_fullness, hem_drop)`: four teacher-seeded landmarks now distinguish sloping shoulders from a visibly narrower curved hem. v1's upper span 172 and hem span 176 produced the reported broad block; v2 ratios are derived from the teacher window, not the image-difference metric.
- `sleeve_cap_at_shoulder(shoulder_join, cap_apex, tube_outer_seam, tube_inner_seam)` plus `sleeve_tube(cap_inner,cap_outer,cuff_outer,cuff_inner)`: the right bodice shoulder anchor is the sleeve-cap join; both cap seams join the separately named upper-arm sleeve tube; the tube continues to the cuff opening. This expresses the observed sleeve-cap→tube→cuff topology, instead of treating the puff as one detached appendage.
- `visible_neck_context(top_left,top_right,base_right,base_left)`: a small teacher-observed skin patch inside `(196,38)-(276,130)`; not face, hair, hidden anatomy or full-character expansion.
- `collar_leaf(outer_seat,fold_top,fold_return,inner_lower)`: separate four-anchor left/right folds frame the sampled skin opening. The dark neckline line follows the two leaves/bodice relation; there is no single panel acting as a bag lid.
- `gathered_bow(knot, lobe_tip)`: two anchors for each unequal lobe; generated stations form a shaped leaf along the knot-to-tip axis. `bow_tail(knot, tail_tip)` likewise tapers and turns from a knot relation. Separate centers, tips and width/turn ratios generate the left/right lobes and tails, then a four-cardinal-point knot. Fold lines follow each axis. No dense trace is copied.
- `cloth_flow_seam(start,end,curve_bias,width_ratio)`: two semantic endpoints plus a named tension/bias parameter; emits only a few selected seam/fold lines that explain cloth pull. A seam is kept off silhouettes unless it is the real observed trim/edge.

The crop source anchors in `r1_scene.py` are the small editable input set (four per silhouette family maximum); all extra boundary samples are generated from those parameters and remain teacher measurements, not frozen contours. A includes fitted bodice, shoulder-cap-to-sleeve-tube adjacency, cuff, paired collar leaves, only source-visible neck context, attached chest bow/tails, and selected folds; no hair/hand/full-pose reconstruction is claimed.

## B — original open-palm support action

B is independently invented and does not inspect/use A's teacher assets. The intended successful action is **an upturned articulated hand supporting a small shallow fruit dish from below**, with a palm heel connected to a bent forearm, thumb bracing one side of the dish, and grouped fingers curling under and around its near rim. The dish footprint and hand/prop support anchors are declared in world space. The hand silhouette, thumb, finger group and dish lip create a clear negative-space notch and front/back interleave. The two-bone IK/taper output is only the connected sleeve/forearm skeleton, not a hand-art solution.

- `two_bone_sleeve(shoulder, wrist_target, upper_length, fore_length, bend_branch, widths)`: existing local `construct_limb` reuses semantic IK/taper, whose method provenance is vendor `R.ik2` joint solving and `R.taper` endpoint-width construction in `rig.js`; no vendor art, identity, coordinates or frames. Compact curve handles are sampled from the generated taper output.
- `scalloped_palm_foot_support(wrist,foot_left,foot_center,foot_right)`: four semantic anchors generate one closed, broad upturned palm/heel surface. Its support edge is fitted to the sampled dish-foot contour over a continuous span. Three shallow scallops and two valleys build a grouped short-finger silhouette; the heel-to-left-foot contour makes the thumb an integral lobe. Two short web grooves clarify grouping; line marks do not replace a hand silhouette.
- `shallow_dish(rim_left,rim_right,foot_right,foot_left)`: four generated family anchors produce a broad shallow vessel with an elliptical rim, small flat foot and fruit/leaf still life. The dish's two-dimensional foot points are derived from the same final guarded contour that is rendered; the project resolves the registration and the renderer's depth derives all hand/prop front/back visibility.
- `gesture_variants`: baseline supports the dish at chest level; the successful variant raises the dish toward the shoulder and changes dish width by +12% while preserving three finger and thumb contacts; the labeled near-miss control keeps all hand geometry unchanged but moves only the dish by 0.34 scene units, leaving an explicit support gap. All cases use the same camera. Names/ratios are authored experiment choices, not pose observations.

Every family returns named closed contours and semantic line owners on named `SupportFrame`s. Existing v1 smooth sampling/guard, support projection, triangle visibility, and visible-line clipping remain the only runtime path. Structural preflight validates the final hand silhouette, exact wrist-to-forearm attachment, the palm's sampled left/center/right foot contacts with the dish, unchanged-hand near miss, and actual support-depth crossings. It is only a scene-specific surface/footprint check, not general volume physics. Depth derives every front/back cut; no manual visibility intervals. This does not certify art legibility; actual v2 images remain independently reviewed.
