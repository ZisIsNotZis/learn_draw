  With `AXIS_UNDER_Z = -100` (below `FOOT_Z = -95`) `dish.derived_rings()` reports **foot radius 0.00** and every `contact.underside_ring` sample carries a **zero normal** (`max|normal| = 0.000e+00` at t = 0.0/0.5/1.0); `util.underside_heights` then computes `(v − surf) · 0 = 0.0` exactly for every vertex.
