## Low-dimensional artist parameters

`source/semantic-parameters.json` is the frozen editable family interface. It lists observation IDs and roles for every source-backed station, then separate `artist_parameterization` / `generated_structural_hypothesis` values. Crop location seeds are fields/neighborhoods, not exact edges. Only a few station coordinates are hand-entered per family; radii, widths, controls, curve samples and support projection are relation-generated. Approximate widths and closure controls express the intended crop-scale composition and deliberately asymmetric garment components. They are not pixel-metric fits or measured hidden construction.

The observed edge notes support only their named visible neighborhoods; no source geometry is copied into runtime. One teacher-only measurement pass records only four accepted sparse teacher-pointer intervals: bodice-left (108.52px open annotation length, 102.88px chord, descending/inward with last turn); bodice-lower (107.37px open length, 105px chord, shallow interval); left loop edge (68.01px open arc); right loop edge (71.69px open arc with opposite turning). The bounds/curvature categories and caveats are in `teacher-measurements/station-measurements.json`; the left/right values do not measure whole lobe sizes or establish exact pixel edges. That teacher-only artifact is excluded from runtime. Do not create/reuse a dense contour, patch sampler, region mask, target-derived pixel table or an offset lookup marketed as derived geometry.

## Scene and rendering boundary

The reference-frame design crop is 400×480; root background color denotes nearby field, not inferred subject material. Body, skin, collar, sleeve, cuff, bow and unknown-identity neighbor are named components with support IDs and finite depth gradients. All fills are triangulated from generated contours and passed together to existing depth-derived compound visibility. Every authored silhouette/interior mark is separately mapped through the same world support and clipped by `visible_segment_spans`. Collar/sleeve/cuff/bow joins are local artist attachments/overlays, not verified physical seams or a global garment/anatomy model. No manual visibility ranges/cuts, custom backend, or new physics are introduced.

Builder accepts only a local parameter record and an output path; rendering accepts only the generated SVG. Neither accepts/opens the source image, crop, regions, point patches, landmarks, traces, or teacher manifest. The registered crop is supplied only to the external `scripts/draw check` comparison after generator output is frozen. Check metrics are alarm-only and may not drive edits. Writer does not inspect/judge output images; Main owns the later fresh actual-image review.

## Required distinction / current non-claims

- **source_observation:** part identity / finite color field, neighborhood, local direction or contact described in the teacher-v2 whitelist.
- **artist_parameterization:** smooth closed outlines, neck shape, collar extents, shoulder join, sleeve bulge/width taper, cuff contact, loop extents, tail width and generated visible truncation.
- **generated_structural_hypothesis:** occluded closure, bodice/sleeve join, cuff attach, leaf underside, bow knot overlap and depth ordering.

No hidden armhole, sleeve seam, exact hem or full neck/cuff circumference; no blue-strip identity, pink-tail endpoint, unknown-tail continuation, complete portrait, invented hand, teacher PASS as art PASS, or R1 acceptance. Never use N1/BD1/BD4/TD1 or LTI2/full left-tail centerline as verified seeds. Do not access/render B or sealed heldout.
