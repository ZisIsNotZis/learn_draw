## Interface contract for a later generator

1. Keep semantic authoring parameters named and few-dimensional: station extents, orientation, width/height ratios, opening tilt, and local curvature controls. The table above gives **estimate slots**, not approved numeric values or final boundaries. If numeric bounds are encoded later, label them `teacher_estimate` with their crop stations, source hashes, confidence, and UNKNOWN intervals.
2. Use visible local region/trace annotations only to constrain a low-dimensional editable family and explain where it comes from. Do not feed a dense contour, segmentation mask, patch image, RGB histogram, or crop into runtime/rendering. Teacher analysis may be detailed; final geometry remains generated from semantic parameters, not traced raster vertices.
3. Distinguish three claim classes in fitting docs/data: `source_observation` (directly visible field/transition/neighborhood), `artist_parameterization` (regularized shape/width/curvature choices), and `generated_structural_hypothesis` (closure, attachment/shared seam or unseen continuation required by the generator). A hidden/shared seam with no source-visible pixels cannot be called verified, teacher-derived, or supported.
4. Where the observed edge terminates at overlap/occlusion/crop or its identity is unclear, keep a bounded unknown region or stop the supported segment. No crop edge becomes a hem, no pink/blue contact becomes an endpoint/material identity, no opening sample becomes complete anatomy, and no cap-to-tube construction partition becomes an inked seam.
5. Prior to any new family fit or artifact change, Main must route a fresh semantic reviewer to check this reconstruction against the exact registered source. This note itself does not reopen fit authorization or pass A/art/R1.
