## A — teacher-fitted upper garment families

Teacher artifacts `teacher-crop-registered.png`, `teacher-crop-focus.png`, `teacher-grid.png`, `teacher-region-windows.png`, and `teacher-observations.json` document correct source/crop registration and approximate viewing windows. They are labeled **rough observations**, not masks or contours. The visual crop shows a shaped light central garment panel, an attached puff sleeve with a dark cuff, cool folded collar and dark angled neckline, plus an asymmetric pink bow with gathered center and descending tails. Runtime accepts only grammar values and generated geometry; it imports no observation script, image, crop, mask, or trace.

All A coordinates are crop-local pixels, y-up in authored scene; projection maps art to the registered crop view without resize. Four-point caps measure **hand-authored coordinate pairs that actually enter family constructors**, not output curve samples. Named ratios/offsets are editable geometry rules; all output curves are generated and guarded.

- `fitted_bodice_panel(neck_left, neck_right, hem_right, hem_left, side_fullness, hem_drop)`: four teacher-seeded relational anchors from neckline/shoulder, lateral bodice, and lower crop. Generated sides interpolate from shoulder to waist with asymmetric ease; bottom is a shallow curved hem; neckline bows between endpoints. Role: recognizable shaped garment mass rather than rectangular blob. Side fullness and hem-drop are family-shape parameters, not traced boundary pixels.
- `sleeve_cap_and_taper(shoulder_join, cap_apex, cuff_outer, cuff_inner)`: four anchors encode the observed right shoulder/cap, sleeve volume, and cuff opening. A rounded asymmetric cap flows to a narrowing opening; a derived cuff band shares the generated cuff edge. Local width determines cap bulge and taper; cuff width is a rule ratio, not another coordinate.
- `layered_collar(neck_left, collar_apex, collar_outer, lower_fold)`: four relational anchors. Generates pale folded collar and inset/edge following the upper chest angle; paired fold strokes follow those seams. No flat horizontal strip or fabricated neck/pose claim.
- `gathered_bow(knot, lobe_tip)`: two anchors for each unequal lobe; generated stations form a shaped leaf along the knot-to-tip axis. `bow_tail(knot, tail_tip)` likewise tapers and turns from a knot relation. Separate centers, tips and width/turn ratios generate the left/right lobes and tails, then a four-cardinal-point knot. Fold lines follow each axis. No dense trace is copied.
- `cloth_flow_seam(start,end,curve_bias,width_ratio)`: two semantic endpoints plus a named tension/bias parameter; emits only a few selected seam/fold lines that explain cloth pull. A seam is kept off silhouettes unless it is the real observed trim/edge.

The crop source anchors in `r1_scene.py` are the small editable input set (four per silhouette family maximum); all extra boundary samples are generated from those parameters. A has a complete upper-garment composition with bodice, collar, bow, sleeve/cuff adjacency and selected folds; no hair/hand/pose is claimed.

## B — original open-palm support action

B is independently invented and does not inspect/use A's teacher assets. The intended successful action is **an upturned articulated hand supporting a small shallow fruit dish from below**, with a palm heel connected to a bent forearm, thumb bracing one side of the dish, and grouped fingers curling under and around its near rim. The dish footprint and hand/prop support anchors are declared in world space. The hand silhouette, thumb, finger group and dish lip create a clear negative-space notch and front/back interleave. The two-bone IK/taper output is only the connected sleeve/forearm skeleton, not a hand-art solution.

- `two_bone_sleeve(shoulder, wrist_target, upper_length, fore_length, bend_branch, widths)`: existing local `construct_limb` reuses semantic IK/taper, whose method provenance is vendor `R.ik2` joint solving and `R.taper` endpoint-width construction in `rig.js`; no vendor art, identity, coordinates or frames. Compact curve handles are sampled from the generated taper output.
- `articulated_palm_body(wrist,palm_center,width_ratio)`: two anchor pairs and a named width create an asymmetric heel/knuckle panel; the wrist attaches to the forearm and separate digit roots meet its knuckle edge.
- `pointed_support_digit(root,tip,width)` generates the complete tapered contour of each index/middle/ring finger, with taper ending at an exact foot contact and separated silhouette gaps. The index, middle, and ring are distinct components, not short finger-line marks. `tapered_thumb_brace(root,bend,left_foot_contact,width)` adds an opposing fully filled curved thumb; it is a separate attached silhouette with a named support touch.
- `shallow_dish(rim_left,rim_right,foot_right,foot_left)`: four generated family anchors produce a broad shallow vessel with an elliptical rim, small flat foot and fruit/leaf still life. The dish's two-dimensional foot points are derived from the same final guarded contour that is rendered; the project resolves the registration and the renderer's depth derives all hand/prop front/back visibility.
- `gesture_variants`: baseline supports the dish at chest level; the successful variant raises the dish toward the shoulder and changes dish width by +12% while preserving three finger and thumb contacts; the labeled near-miss control keeps all hand geometry unchanged but moves only the dish by 0.34 scene units, leaving an explicit support gap. All cases use the same camera. Names/ratios are authored experiment choices, not pose observations.

Every family returns named closed contours and semantic line owners on named `SupportFrame`s. The existing revision-v1 smooth sampler/guard, support projection, triangle visibility, and visible-line clipping are reused through revision-local composition glue. Structural preflight validates actual final contours, semantic limb/palm joints, exact world-space contacts at three finger tips and thumb foot, control separation, and positive-area crossings of each actual case's affine support footprints. It makes no general volume-physics claim. Geometry determines every front/back cut; no manual visibility intervals. This evidence cannot certify art legibility; actual images remain independently reviewed.
