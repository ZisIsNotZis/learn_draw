## Root classification

**Geometry support legality is not original art completion.** `scene.yaml` and construction code implement semantic attachments/support frames/visibility; these establish representable geometry and some numeric relations, not component identity, attractive/readable silhouettes, convincing joints, contact cues, or purposeful line/color design. Do not reopen the now-repaired renderer topology/depth issue to address art defects.

**A — garment semantics replaced by generic masses.** `work/render_r1.py::observed_study()` creates the bodice, shoulder, sleeve, and bow as ellipses or sparse polygon shapes on generic supports (`_ellipse(Point(0,0),1.0,1.08,48)`, shoulder ellipse, sleeve ellipse, ellipse knot). The observed crop in root `image.jpg` has an asymmetrical fitted light garment: a shoulder/sleeve cap joining a sloped neckline/shoulder seam, curved sleeve-to-bodice boundary, dark curved neckline/trim, a distinct chest bow with two lobes/tails and knot, and visible seams/folds. Separate overlapping oval masses do not encode sleeve opening/cuff, garment panel boundary, collar/neckline construction, fabric fold direction, or bow silhouette; support binding and plausible overlap alone therefore read as geometry, not clothing. The public scope correctly limits imitation to visible shoulder/upper torso; no inferred hand/pose is allowed. This diagnosis is about missing 2D authored shape grammar, not a unique underlying 3D reconstruction.

**B — human/action semantics are too primitive.** `scene.yaml` correctly names torso, upper_arm, forearm, hand, prop, supports, two-bone IK and taper, but body/head/hand/prop use generated ellipses; arm segments are smooth tapers; `render_r1.py` adds only simple finger marks. A palm ellipse plus strokes has no rule for palm heel/thumb-root wedge, thumb opposing the prop, separated finger wraps/over-under order, knuckle rhythm, or a grasp silhouette that differentiates holding from nearby shapes. Tapered connected segments don't create an elbow's outer point/inner crease, sleeve cuff overlap, compressed/extended cloth folds, or a deliberate joint transition. Exact shared anchor/tangent and plane legality cannot substitute for legible hand-prop contact: contact needs a visible wrap/overlap cue and coherent silhouette. No transferable detailed hand/finger/sleeve/joint/prop drawing rule has yet been demonstrated by the selected vendor method: the method card supports semantic IK plus segment taper, explicitly does not supply hand/finger art, and cautions that taper is not elbow anatomy. Use those methods only for arm anchors and generated limb widths, not as a hand grammar. These are 2D authored design choices; the 2D reference cannot determine a unique 3D truth.

**Framing is not head fit.** The B public camera promises head-to-mid-torso-plus-prop. Current head is a generated ellipse (scene component), but `render_r1.py::render_panel()` computes panel fit from projected `component.outline` values in the `parts` collection; render content is clipped to the panel's artwork rect. This is a fill/outline extent fit, not a semantic head/hat/hair or composition fit; authored marks/extra head contour can extend beyond those extents or be clipped. Any observed head crop should be checked against B source ownership/actual clip rectangle, not fixed by changing engine framing blindly. A's limited reference scope does not require a head reconstruction.

## Bounded route comparison / choice

1. **Recommended — semantic garment and limb silhouette grammar on existing support/mapping pipeline.** For A, replace oval stand-ins with named garment regions generated from compact relations: bodice as a neckline-to-waist panel with shoulder/side edges and a curved lower edge; separate asymmetric sleeve cap/wedge attached at shoulder, sleeve tube taper ending in a cuff/opening; neckline/collar trim as a curved band inset from neckline; bow as paired lobes + narrow tails + knot whose joins derive from chest anchor; 2–3 intentional seam/fold strokes following panel tension/edge direction. Use only frozen crop features as reference-based inputs, close reference before render, and do not trace dense boundaries. For B, preserve semantic IK targets/supports; construct clothed torso/head within frame, upper and forearm sleeves as elbow-joined taper silhouettes with an authored elbow bend/fold and cuff overlap; hand as palm wedge, opposing thumb wedge and three/four grouped finger bands generated from wrist-to-grip frame, with explicit near/far occlusion/contact cue around one simple mug/prop silhouette and handle or rim contact. Keep baseline/variant semantics from freeze, label same awkward control, but ensure its miss is visibly distinct. Coordinates are relation/generated, <=4 hand-authored coordinates per shape. Intent comments/names for every shape/layer. Existing depth visibility remains authoritative; no hand-coded occlusion cuts.
2. **Alternative — spend effort on new surface/body modeling or vendor-method research first.** This might clarify articulation or surface attachment, but the architecture already represents supports and the observed failure is authored silhouette/shape grammar. Further engine/vendor research would not add missing clothing, finger, cuff, or contact drawing rules and risks repeating a solved technical lane. Not recommended.

No claim that route 1 guarantees readability. It directly addresses diagnosed missing semantics and is the only in-scope useful next construction hypothesis.
