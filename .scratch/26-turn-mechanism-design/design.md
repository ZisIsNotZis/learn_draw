# Return-bend turn mechanism — revised current design

## Current evidence (three causal observations)

1. Exercise24's `.35` transverse camber and Exercise25's fixed apparent-contour cue did not clarify the frozen axial half-turn; the latter is a negative perception result for its cases, not all apparent contours or curvature.
2. Axial width-frame twist can make a positive material width project edge-on. A center-route bend changes the actual surface normal while its transverse material-width axis remains fixed, so it is a different causal construction.
3. The teacher image contains flowing curled/bent bands, but one stylized view cannot decide whether any band twists, curls, folds, or specifies unique 3D. It supports a shape-grammar experiment, not 3D recovery. The image was not traced or given to a renderer.

## Responsibility boundary

Local (u,v) art and tested opaque-depth visibility already exist in the component architecture. General semantic line selection/selective art projection remains unaccepted and belongs to later S2; it is not silently added here. The proposed test keeps simple local fill/stripe. If success requires hand visibility intervals, front/back duplicates, screen-space patches, special contour coaching, or another output modality, stop rather than widen architecture. No native image generation, guarantee, art/S1a pass, or implementation authorization is implied.

## One recommendation: return-bend support, with pinned fixtures

A small support-family study may be considered after parent authorization. Keep every prior axial case/spec/image unchanged as a control. The purpose is to test a drawing-friendly support grammar under `2D artwork + 3D support`, not inverse-recover a unique reference surface.

Proposed next API: `scripts/ribbon_return.py`, `build_return_mesh(spec: ReturnSpec, u_samples: int, v_samples: int) -> ReturnMesh`. `ReturnSpec` contains root `C0`, root tangent `e_x`, bend-plane normal `W`, route length `L`, root material width `w`, `tip_ratio`, and turn angle `alpha`; derive `e_d = W × e_x`. It has no camera, color, image path, mesh vertices, or visibility ranges.

For `u ∈ [0,1]`, `q(u)=3u²−2u³`, `theta=alpha*q(u)`, `T(u)=cos(theta)e_x + sin(theta)e_d`, and `C(u)=C0+L∫₀ᵘT(t)dt`. The support is `P(u,v)=C(u)+0.5*v*w*(1-(1-tip_ratio)u)*W`, `v∈[-1,1]`. `W` stays fixed; longitudinal width-frame twist is zero. Use fixed deterministic quadrature. Material width is `w(u)`; orthographic projected width is `w(u)*sqrt(1-(D·W)^2)`. For the bounded domain below, `|P_u×P_v|=L*w(u)/2>0`; monotone route turn through at most `pi` gives an injective planar center route. These are formula guarantees, not a claim that every finite projected triangulation or visibility case is sound.

### Analytic selection and numerical preflight

The preregistered primary positive fixture is: `C0=(0,0,0)`, `L=3`, `w=.36`, `tip_ratio=1`, `alpha=pi`; frame `e_x=(1,0,0)`, `e_d=(0,1,0)`, `W=(0,0,1)` with `e_x×e_d=W`. Primary orthographic camera points toward the viewer along `D+=(0,cos10°,sin10°)`, with screen-right `R=(1,0,0)` and screen-up `U=D+×R=(0,sin10°,-cos10°)`, so `R×U=D+`. This fixed slight out-of-plane view breaks the rejected symmetric projection while retaining branch overlap.

Analytically, `theta(1-u)=pi-theta(u)` and the paired stations `u=.25,.75` share screen-right position, have opposite facing signs, and are not edge-on (`|(T×W)·D+|=.8685229`). Their centerline separation in the depth-axis coordinate is bounded by `[.707095,1.5]` world units; their screen offset is `[.122786,.260472]`, below projected width `.354531`. The minimum exposed strip width is the minimum screen offset `.122786`; `.094059` is the minimum remaining overlap width at the maximum screen offset, not exposed margin. At equal projected points their camera-depth ordering differs by at least `.718003`. Thus the geometry predicts both an actual overlap and exposed area on both opposing-facing branches; a normal-sign flip alone is not the criterion.

## Bounded contract and one preregistered study

Require finite values; normalize `e_x,W`, reject `|e_x·W|>1e-10`, derive `e_d=W×e_x`, verify the full right-handed orthonormal frame within `1e-10`. Require unit camera `R,U,D`, mutually orthogonal within `1e-10`, and `R×U=D`. Width is at least `.05` world units and the formula Jacobian at least `.025` square units in this domain. Reject zero/negative dimensions and any invalid frame. Finite mesh sampling is `16≤Nu≤32`, `2≤Nv≤4`, `2*Nu*Nv≤256` faces; predeclare sampling and do not raise it to repair a failed visual case.
